---
title: "Take a data tour"
description: "Use when the user is new and asks what data is available, what they can ask, to be walked through the data, for an introduction or onboarding to the data, or for a demo of what the agent can do."
---
Walk a new user through the business and the data in a few short steps. Keep it light and visual.

1. Start with two sentences on who we are and what the data covers (company, markets, and the period the order history spans), based on the business context.
2. Run three quick queries on `orders_transactions` and show each with a one-line explanation of what it means:
   - Total Sale Price (revenue), Order Count and Average Order Value over the whole history as KPIs.
   - Revenue by month for the last fiscal year in the data, as a line chart.
   - Revenue by country, top 8, as a bar chart.
3. Explain in plain words which areas the user can explore: Orders Transactions (sales and fulfillment), Customers (who buys and how they found us), Products and Inventory (catalog and stock). Do not mention Web Events as a source for analysis.
4. Offer exactly three example questions the user can click or type next, each one answerable from the data, for example one about growth, one about customers and one about products.
5. Never show emails, addresses or postal codes.
