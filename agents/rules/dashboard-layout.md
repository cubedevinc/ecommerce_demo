type: agent_requested
description: Use when the user asks to build, redesign or lay out a dashboard, overview, scorecard or report page, and the question is how to organize it (what goes where, how many charts, how to order them).
---
When building a dashboard, organize it for a reader who scans from top to bottom. Do not choose colors or fonts; those come from the style book.

- **Top row:** 3 to 5 KPI tiles with the headline numbers (for example revenue, orders, average order value), each with a clear title and a comparison to the previous period when one exists.
- **Second section:** one trend chart over time, full width, so the story of the period is visible at a glance.
- **Middle sections:** breakdowns that explain the trend, two charts side by side (for example by department and by country). Use bar charts for rankings and categories, line charts only for time.
- **Bottom:** one detail table for readers who want exact numbers.
- **Limits:** no more than 8 to 10 widgets per page. If there is more, split it into tabs by topic (for example Sales, Customers, Products) instead of one long page.
- **Filters:** put a date filter first, then at most two others, above everything else. Default the date filter to the latest fiscal period in the data.
- **Titles:** every widget has a short title that states what it shows, in business words, and the dashboard has a one-line purpose at the top.
- **Order:** most important and broadest first, most specific last. Keep related charts next to each other and use the same time range and filters across the page.
- After building, tell the user in one line how the page is organized and offer one change, such as adding a tab.
